# Hero cinematic campaign entrance

Scope: animation only. Existing Hero body markup, layout/typography/content, header navigation markup, photographs, color tokens, social controls, booking behavior and every other section are unchanged. No dependencies were added. The site already uses native CSS/JavaScript; this addition follows that implementation and the transform/opacity/batched-work principles in the GSAP Performance skill without installing GSAP.

Runtime files: hero-entry.css and hero-entry.js; index.html only adds their head references and a small prepaint fail-open bootstrap. Two decorative, aria-hidden, pointer-transparent nodes (atmosphere and dust canvas) are created at runtime inside the Hero. They are absolutely positioned and do not contribute to layout.

Sequence (about 2.05 seconds):
- Pure-black Hero curtain before paint; header/content remain in the DOM. The standalone bootstrap fails open after 700ms if the enhancement cannot start. Reduced motion, direct section hashes and restored scroll positions skip this blackout.
- When the module is ready, image decode may receive at most 80ms grace. The curtain starts clearing at 40ms over 700ms. Existing background images stay unmodified and retain their existing is-active/crossfade behavior. Their parent performs an extremely small uniform push-in from .992 to 1 over 2050ms.
- Header and eyebrow fade at 240ms; eyebrow rises 9px. Headline lines clip/reveal upward 14px at 430/560/690ms, each over 780ms. End clipping includes extra room for Vietnamese diacritics.
- Existing supporting copy and CTA rise/fade at 1130ms over 520ms. Existing social controls fade at 1360ms over 420ms. At 2050ms all entrance classes are removed, restoring the exact existing composition with no continued text/CTA animation.
- A low-alpha amber radial light sits behind the model/hands. It settles into a nearly imperceptible 24-second opacity/translation cycle.

Dust/performance: 14 tiny gold points on desktop, 5 on mobile, only in the dark margins; no glitter, additive bloom, textures, bokeh or shadow blur. Radius .35–.71 CSS px, point alpha .055–.109, canvas opacity .65. Drawing is capped at 20fps; backing buffer is capped at 1.8 million pixels and DPR 1.25. Off-screen/covered scenes, page visibility, dialogs/menu and BFCache suspend work. No-canvas support retains the CSS lighting and complete UI.

Cursor: a separate parent translate follows an eased target of at most ±1.5px horizontally / ±1px vertically. It activates after the entrance on fine-pointer desktop devices only. It never changes image crop, model proportions, the active image, layout or content transforms. Pointer leave returns it to zero; covered/hidden/reduced states reset it. Motion classes sit on the backdrop rather than the Hero so they do not reset the existing 8-second image timer.

Interaction/lifecycle: pointer, keyboard or focus access immediately completes the entrance without cancelling the event. Starting a section transition, leaving the scene, resizing or hiding the tab also exposes the completed Hero; reentry never replays. There are no wheel/touchmove/scroll handlers or forced scroll writes in the new module. Initial ResizeObserver delivery does not cancel the entrance. Real teardown removes listeners, observers, timers, RAF and decorative nodes. prefers-reduced-motion shows the full original Hero immediately and removes ambient/cursor motion.

Validation: new modeled checks pass for prepaint fallback, deep links, timing/once-only state, initial resize, bounded decode, early access/section escape, particle/DPR budgets, cursor bounds, pause/resume, reduced motion, no-canvas fallback and teardown. Existing Hero image-cycle, 900ms section gesture navigation, Treatments entrance and Gallery entrance checks pass. The Gallery test fixture was limited to its current authored controls; an assertion for a previously undone CTA is no longer unconditional. Hash verification confirms all prior CSS/JS, imagery and Hero/body markup remain byte-identical. Live browser rendering remains unverified because of the prior browser access restriction; validation here is source and modeled behavior, not a screenshot review.
